The woman behind the camera

I photograph love.
I just prefer it
slightly haunted.

Wednesday Addams is a fine-art wedding photographer based in New York, available wherever the light is interesting and the guest list is suspicious.

Her work is a study in contrast: tenderness against stone, joy against shadow, a white dress in a room that has seen things. She believes the best photographs are the ones that feel like memories before they happen.
